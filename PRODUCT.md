# Riggs HD Product Contract

## Product thesis

Riggs HD helps young people use basketball as an entry point into relationships, guidance, opportunity, and a stronger future.

The site's unique mechanism is a visible pathway:

**SPORT → RELATIONSHIP → GUIDANCE → OPPORTUNITY → FUTURE**

This is not a basketball-training website with generic life-skills copy attached. It is a holistic-development nonprofit experience in which athletics earns attention and trust, while education, career exposure, mentorship, financial literacy, and personal growth carry the mission forward.

## Primary audiences

1. Students and student-athletes
2. Parents and families
3. Donors and sponsors
4. Mentors and professionals
5. Schools and community partners

## Visitor promise

Within the first viewport, a first-time visitor should understand:

- what Riggs HD does;
- why basketball is only the beginning;
- which action is available to them now.

## Brand architecture commitment

- Riggs HD is the primary mission identity.
- Destined To Be Great / DTBG is a major basketball-development identity in the experience.
- The legal relationship is not stated until the owner confirms it.
- The original logo is preserved as brand history and source material; the interface may use a clearer typographic expression at small sizes.

## Truth and evidence rules

- Preserve content and functions from the public Wix site and its linked public services.
- Do not invent stats, testimonials, partners, dates, pricing, eligibility, outcomes, tax claims, or organizational relationships.
- The only homepage impact count currently verified on the public source is “30 students and entrepreneurs helped so far.” If used, it must be framed as a current-site figure pending date-range confirmation.
- Past activities remain visible as past work, never as upcoming events.
- Unresolved facts use a visible `CONTENT REQUIRED` state in project data and documentation; they are not dressed up as public claims.

## Required primary actions

- Explore Programs
- Register / Express Interest
- Donate
- Sponsor a Student
- Become a Mentor
- Volunteer / Speak
- Partner
- Contact

Donate remains a globally visible action.

## Functional contract

- Responsive global navigation and mobile menu
- Program discovery with explicit status and working actions
- Events with current, upcoming, registration-open, closed, past, and draft states
- Donation handoff to the confirmed current provider
- Training, consultation, and speaking request handoffs to the existing active forms
- Impact and story archive using original public media
- Contact pathway that works without a hidden or fake backend
- Accessible keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion support, and semantic content
- SEO/social metadata and a deployable static production build

## Visual direction commitment

The owner approved **Locker Room → Lecture Hall**, using the **Layered Dossier** homepage composition in `.impeccable/mocks/home/option-a.png` as the build law.

- Execution is comp-led: the approved first viewport governs topology, overlap, scale, material, and visual hierarchy.
- The gym photograph remains the emotional field; the warm advising dossier physically enters it from the right.
- One amber court line becomes the five-stage pathway across the fold.
- The interface uses deep navy, cobalt, warm cream, and court amber; court lacquer, manila folders, taped photographs, and whiteboard ink form the component language.
- Generated concept copy that is not verified by the Wix source will not be literalized in the build.
- The system must extend across all routes without falling back to a generic card grid.
