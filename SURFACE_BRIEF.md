# Homepage Surface Brief

- World: **Locker Room → Lecture Hall**

## Composition contract

The grayscale gym photograph occupies the left and center and remains visually dominant. The warm advising dossier enters from the right foreground with visible physical depth. The headline is integrated into the gym field rather than placed in a generic hero panel. A court-amber line crosses the gym floor, reaches the dossier, and continues as the five-stage pathway rail along the lower edge. Donate remains global in the dark navigation; Explore Programs is the first local action.

Generated phrases inside the comp are not source truth. Only verified website content or clearly labeled pending content may ship.

## Component grammar

- Corners: mostly square; small 2–6px easing on interactive controls; rounded shapes only for physical photo corners and compact tags.
- Rules: 1px navy/cream rules; 3–5px amber route line where it carries the pathway.
- Elevation: dossier casts one soft offset shadow; internal surfaces use seams or material contrast, not stacked shadow cards.
- Type: very condensed, heavy display face; readable humanist sans for body; monospace for status/date microcopy; handwritten face only for short verified annotations.
- Navigation: dark fixed field, typographic wordmark, direct route labels, rectangular cobalt Donate control.
- States: cobalt for active/action, amber for pathway/current status, muted navy/cream for inactive, red reserved for closed/error.
