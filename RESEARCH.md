## B. What is outdated or should be archived

- Summer Hoops University 2025 is past. Its listed dates ran from May through August 2025. It must not appear as an upcoming registration opportunity in 2026.
- The 2024–25 Pacers career-exploration program is past and its form requires Google sign-in. It belongs in the impact/archive layer, not the current-events CTA layer.
- The Givebutter campaign is live but its visible goal refers to a Fall 2023 scholarship. Keep the donation capability, but do not present the campaign text as a current 2026 goal without confirmation.
- The Join Us donation link `https://givebutter.com/RkLIVG` returns 404 and should be removed.
- The Projects page contains event flyers from 2018, 2022, and 2023. These are useful as historical evidence, but not as current promotions.
- Footer copyright is frozen at 2016 and should become dynamic.
- The basketball-training form and some historical graphics reference old dates, prices, addresses, phone numbers, and the domain `destinedtobeagreat.net` / `riggshdprosinc.com`. These are not treated as current facts.

## E. Brand architecture

Recommended architecture:

- **Riggs HD** is the primary mission identity and site owner: holistic development through athletics, education, relationships, and career exposure.
- **Destined To Be Great (DTBG)** is presented as a major basketball-development program or initiative within the visitor experience.
- Until ownership confirms the legal relationship, the site copy will avoid language such as “division,” “subsidiary,” or “program of.” It will use neutral language such as “Riggs HD and Destined To Be Great work across...” where needed.

The current logo is preserved as a provenance asset. It is visually dense and does not scale well in navigation, so the rebuild may use a clean typographic Riggs HD wordmark in the interface while retaining the original mark in About, history, and identity contexts.

## G. Priority user journeys

### Student or student-athlete

Land on the mission → see the pathway beyond basketball → compare programs → choose training or college/career support → open the appropriate interest form.

### Parent or family member

Understand who the program is for → see what holistic development includes → review program status and expectations → contact or register.

### Donor or sponsor

Understand the mission → see verified work and the honest evidence boundary → choose Donate or Sponsor a Student → reach the current Givebutter campaign or contact pathway.

### Mentor, speaker, or professional

See how professional exposure fits the model → understand available roles → submit a speaking/mentor/volunteer interest request.

### School or community partner

See workshop and program capabilities → understand the mission and founder credibility → start a partnership conversation by email/contact.

## H. Design-system direction

The visual world should feel like **a community gym opening into a college and career advising office**—not a generic sports brand and not a corporate nonprofit template.

Core system commitments:

- real photography leads;
- one durable blue/black/cream identity with a warm court-orange or scholarship-gold action accent;
- editorial hierarchy with a strong condensed display face, a highly readable text face, and monospace microcopy for schedules/statuses;
- geometry drawn from court boundaries, clipboards, advising folders, and program worksheets;
- status labels are explicit and semantic: registration open, interest form, past, closed, and content required;
- repeated “cards” are replaced by more specific artifacts: pathway rows, program bands, status boards, story frames, and advising notes;
- motion is restrained and purposeful: court-line drawing, focus shifts, and path progression; reduced-motion mode remains complete.

Reference-site lessons used without copying:

- Bigger Than Sports: strong mission compression and photography-first storytelling.
- LEAD Center for Youth: program naming and clear outcome-oriented architecture.
- PeacePlayers: clear calls to participate and donate, supported by human photography.
- USA Basketball Foundation: program pillars, direct donation visibility, and partner pathways.
- Up2Us Sports: career readiness treated as a concrete program, not a vague benefit.

## Functional migration notes

- The new site will preserve active services through explicit outbound actions instead of depending on Wix-specific forms, chat, newsletter, or member widgets.
- The generic Wix member page has no evidence of a real member workflow and will not be recreated without requirements.
- Contact will be implemented so it does not pretend to submit to a nonexistent backend. The first version will use a transparent email action or a deploy-safe form provider only after its destination is confirmed.
- Every historical event is modeled with a real status rather than being silently removed or accidentally presented as current.
